Our Company Divisions

We operate through specialized divisions to deliver high-quality power infrastructure projects across the Kingdom.

Electrical Projects Division

Responsible for overhead and underground electrical networks, substations, and street lighting systems.

Civil Projects Division

Handles excavation, foundations, concrete works, roads, and support civil structures for electrical projects.

Procurement Division

Manages sourcing, vendors, supply chain, and timely delivery of materials for project execution.

Technical Support Division

Provides design review, technical documentation, site coordination, and compliance supervision.

Finance & Administration

Oversees budgeting, contracts, auditing, and administrative operations for smooth workflow.

Human Resources

Responsible for recruitment, training, employee development, and workforce management.
