About Us

Building Power Infrastructure That Moves the Kingdom Forward

Who We Are

Delivering Distribution Networks up to 34 kV to Global Standards Across the Kingdom

Saudi Delta Company for Power Projects (SDCP) is a leading contractor in electrical infrastructure delivery within the Kingdom, with 20+ years of experience. We provide end-to-end solutions for MV/LV networks up to 34 kV, including underground and overhead works, supporting civil activities, acceptance and quality testing, and Testing & Commissioning (T&C), with fully documented handover packages, including As-Built and Handover Dossiers, aligned with client requirements, SEC Distribution specifications, and relevant IEC standards.

Our Divisions

A Specialized Operating Model Delivering High-Performance Execution Nationwide

We operate through integrated divisions that combine extensive field experience with disciplined operational management to deliver electrical infrastructure solutions across the Kingdom. Our model is built on rigorous Quality (QA/QC) and Safety (HSE) requirements, supported by effective resource management and supply chain control, ensuring fully documented deliveries aligned with client specifications and international standards.

Electrical Projects Management

SDCP’s Electrical Projects Management is the company’s center of excellence for nationwide delivery of distribution network projects up to 34 kV. We lead field execution for underground and overhead networks and ensure operational readiness through materials and workmanship inspections/approvals, functional verification, and trial energization activities, delivering an engineered, fully documented handover that meets client requirements and SEC/IEC standards.

Civil Projects Management

SDCP’s Civil Projects Management leads the execution of supporting civil works for infrastructure projects through an engineering methodology that ensures quality, safety, and schedule discipline. Our integrated scope includes concrete structures, site works, finishing works, and road reinstatement, with precise control of resources and subcontractors and robust QA/QC and HSE plans. Our experience serves a wide range of projects including public utilities and governmental, educational, healthcare, sports, and commercial facilities across the Kingdom.

Warehousing, Inventory & Field Supply Chain

SDCP’s Warehousing and Materials function manages an integrated system for receiving, inspecting, storing, and issuing materials to project sites in line with the highest quality and safety standards. Key activities include verifying material compliance through Incoming Inspection, implementing Material Coding & Traceability, controlling inventory levels (Min/Max) and periodic stock counts, and preparing materials for dispatch to projects with complete documentation for issues and returns. This model ensures material availability at the right time, reduces waste, and supports faster execution across SDCP projects nationwide.

Engineering & Technical Support

SDCP’s Engineering & Technical Support function provides technical governance across projects nationwide by developing and approving Method Statements and Inspection & Test Plans (ITPs), reviewing Shop Drawings and As-Built documentation, and delivering engineering solutions to site challenges to enhance execution efficiency. The team also supports Acceptance Testing and Commissioning (T&C), conducts Root Cause Analysis (RCA), drives continuous improvement actions, and builds field capability through training and knowledge transfer, ensuring alignment with client requirements and SEC/IEC standards while achieving the highest levels of quality and reliability.
