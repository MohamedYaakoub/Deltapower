Building Power Infrastructure That Moves the Kingdom Forward

Who We Are

Saudi Delta Company for Power Projects is a leading contractor specializing in electrical and civil power infrastructure across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

With decades of expertise and specialized divisions, we deliver safe, reliable power infrastructure solutions across the Kingdom, from planning to final commissioning.

The Technical Support Division delivers specialized expertise through innovation, research, and training, enhancing efficiency, reliability, and best practices across construction and maintenance operations.
