Company Divisions

We operate through specialized divisions to deliver high-quality power infrastructure projects across the Kingdom.

Electrical Project Division

The largest division of Saudi Delta Company, delivering complete electrical infrastructure projects across the Kingdom. It is organized into three main areas:

Unified Contracts: End-to-end management of distribution networks up to 36KV, including design, supply, installation, commissioning, and handover.

Turnkey Projects: Complete project execution, from equipment delivery to operational handover for major clients like SEC and the Ministry of Border Guard.

Maintenance & Operations: Preventive and emergency services to ensure reliable and optimized distribution networks.

Civil Projects Division

Saudi Delta Construction Division provides end-to-end construction services, delivering creative solutions for technically advanced projects. Our expertise spans commercial centers, urban developments, hospitals, hotels, schools, universities, sports facilities, office complexes, government and military buildings, and industrial complexes.

Procurement Division

The Procurement Division ensures timely sourcing of goods and services for projects and internal needs, manages inventories, and oversees surplus materials. Focused on quality, cost efficiency, and timely delivery, it maintains a comprehensive supplier database and continuously improves procurement processes to support company operations locally and internationally.

Technical Support Division

The Technical Support Division provides specialized services to the Construction and Maintenance divisions, ensuring high-quality support to company operations and clients. Through innovation, research, and training, it introduces new technologies and methodologies, improving efficiency, productivity, and service reliability while promoting best practices in construction and safety.
